Why it exists

Turn customer and market needs into a single prioritised set of product requirements that serves as the baseline for the entire program.

These product requirements drive PPA, cost, feasibility, and architecture decisions. Until they are baselined and approved, downstream work remains provisional. Late changes can significantly increase cost and delay the program.

What it delivers
DEF-D1Product requirements document (PRD)

The requirements list built here becomes the body of the PRD. This activity does not contribute to the PRD — it produces its content.
