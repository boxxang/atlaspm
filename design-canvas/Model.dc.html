What breaks

The must-do list and the in-progress list are two lists you reconcile by eye. Steps — the level where work actually moves — carry nothing. And a deliverable is ticked on a third checklist, unconnected to either.

Why the thread sits on the activity, not the step

Real updates span steps. “Design kicked off with the vendor; contact-resistance analysis running alongside” is steps 2 and 3 at once. And 34% of steps run in parallel, so per-step threads would split one week's news into two lists you have to interleave. One thread, one optional step tag, keeps both facts.

The one number the stage list needs

“On step 2 of 8” falls straight out of the ticks — no percentage to invent, no separate status field to keep honest. It is what makes 11 activities scannable in a row each, and it is what the segmented bar draws.

Migration — the real cost

Existing Item{kind:'activity'} rows are not typed against the template, so they cannot be matched automatically. The screen offers a per-row “Looks like TEST-03 · link?” on a title match and leaves the rest alone. Nothing is migrated without a person saying yes.
