Type — one family, seven steps

system-ui throughout at letter-spacing: -0.011em. Numbers get font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums so columns of dates and M/M line up. Georgia is dropped — there is no serif left in the app.

The constraint that outlives the palette

A program has 12–23 stages, set by its profile, not by the code. The sidebar, the timeline rows and the stage list are all authored against the 23-stage built-in for that reason.
