The structural constraint every direction inherits

A programme has 12 to 23 stages, set by its profile, not by the code. Any layout that assumes a stage count — a fixed-height chart, a row of tabs, a grid of cards — breaks on the profile it was not drawn against. The 23-stage built-in is the honest test case, and it is the one every artboard here is drawn with.
