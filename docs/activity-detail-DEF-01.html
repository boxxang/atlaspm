Purpose

Turn what customers and the market are asking for into one ranked, signed requirement set that the whole program is measured against.

Everything downstream is an answer to this list — the PPA targets, the cost model, the feasibility call, the architecture itself. Until it is baselined and signed, every one of those answers is provisional. A requirement that arrives in week 30 costs roughly a hundred times what it costs here, and the difference is paid in silicon.
