UCIe Advanced 64G Die-to-Die PHY IP — Program Flowchart

The full development flow of AtlasPM’s built-in UCIe Advanced 64G Die-to-Die PHY IP template: a die-to-die PHY for UCIe Advanced packages at 64 GT/s — D2D Adapter, Logical PHY and sideband, Electrical PHY, bumps and channel — newly developed, proven on a test chip on an advanced-package test vehicle, and released to the customers who integrate it, from concept to GA. Four workstreams run side by side — digital, electrical PHY, channel and test vehicle, and the customer releases — and join at the test chip tapeout, at silicon validation and at GA. Stages flow top to bottom by start week; columns are the lifecycle bands. Click any stage for its activities, deliverables and links.
